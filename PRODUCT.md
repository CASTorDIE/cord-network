# CORD Network

## Register

brand

## Users

CORD Network serves viewers, community members, and prospective members who want to discover CORD streamers, see who is live, understand what is scheduled next, and follow community events. Visitors may arrive casually from social links or return regularly to check the current schedule.

## Product Purpose

The public website is CORD Network's content-driven community homepage. It should make active streams, upcoming schedules, events, updates, and community activity easy to understand at a glance while giving each item enough context to invite exploration. The code and information architecture should support adding individual member pages later without forcing the homepage into an app-style interface.

## Brand Personality

Creative, modern, and fun. The experience should also feel professional, informative, and energized by exciting community events.

## Anti-references

Avoid stereotypical gamer styling, including aggressive esports motifs, neon overload, faux HUD interfaces, and visual noise. Avoid overly technical or developer-tool aesthetics. Do not turn the public experience into a dashboard or expose the Scheduler's internal editor language. Preserve and refine the established Version 1 visual direction instead of replacing it with an unrelated aesthetic.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with current community activity and useful content.
2. Make excitement legible through hierarchy, pacing, and timely information rather than visual noise.
3. Keep public interactions simple, welcoming, and visitor-focused.
4. Preserve a clear path from the homepage to future member profiles and deeper content.
5. Separate internal publishing tools from the public reading experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, useful screen-reader labels, reduced-motion preferences, and sufficient contrast. Do not communicate event categories or status through color alone. Ensure schedule information remains usable across mobile and desktop layouts and when localized time labels vary in length.
