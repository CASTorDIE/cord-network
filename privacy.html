CORD Network Privacy Policy

CORD Network helps content creators track, discuss, and promote their work. This policy explains what information CORD collects and how we use it.

Information we collect

CORD may collect publicly available information about creators and their activities, such as channel profiles, schedules, posts, announcements, and performance information.

We may also collect information creators submit to CORD or post in the CORD Discord server, including announcements shared through automated bot posts.

The CORD Social Collector retrieves content from configured Discord channels. This can include message text, links, embeds, and references to attachments. It may also download referenced media to local storage. The collector organizes recognizable social accounts and posts into current and weekly Markdown documents for the network’s reference.

How we use and share information

We use this information to support creators, track and discuss their progress, and promote their work. This may include organizing information in CORD tools, creating summaries and reports, and sharing updates or examples in public content across platforms.

Posts in the CORD Discord server may be used publicly, including posts in member-only rooms. If you need to discuss something privately, contact DoM by direct message.

CORD may use AI services to help organize, analyze, and summarize information. Information shared with these services is handled under their respective privacy policies.

Storage and Google Drive

CORD stores collector data on its own systems, including Discord content, downloaded media, generated documents, sync records, and operational logs. Generated documents are also stored in a Google Drive folder for reference.

The collector requests Google Drive’s drive.file permission. This allows it to work with files it creates or files specifically selected or shared with the app. The collector checks information about its configured folder and creates or updates its Markdown documents there. It does not change the folder’s sharing permissions.

People who already have access to the Drive folder may be able to access the documents under its existing sharing settings. Google processes information stored in Drive under its privacy policy.

The connected Google account holder can revoke the collector’s access through their Google Account’s third-party connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access to Drive, but does not automatically delete documents already in Drive or copies stored locally.

Retention

CORD may keep information with no set deletion date. Information may appear publicly for varying lengths of time and may be removed from public view while still kept in CORD’s systems.

The collector does not automatically synchronize later edits or deletions made to the original Discord messages.

Opt-out and removal

If you would like to opt out or request removal of your information, contact DoM directly on Discord at producerdom. CORD will make a reasonable effort to remove your information from its systems.

Website visitors

The CORD Network website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement for more information.

The website requests information from GitHub services and may load creator images from Twitch. These services receive information needed to deliver those resources, such as your IP address. Links on the website may take you to other services, including Twitch, Discord, and Ko-fi, which have their own privacy practices.
